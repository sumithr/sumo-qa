## User prompt

What canonical change classifications does sumo-qa recognise?

## Expected tool

`sumo_qa_load_classifications` (no args).

## Expected use of result

the LLM names the 10 classifications from the returned catalogue (api_contract_change, business_logic_change, security_change, performance_change, frontend_change, infrastructure_change, test_change, docs_change, config_change, data_migration). Doesn't paraphrase from training data.

## Anti-pick

invents classifications from training data; calls `sumo_qa_deciding_approach` (the skill) instead of the loader; calls `sumo_qa_load_principles` (wrong catalogue).
