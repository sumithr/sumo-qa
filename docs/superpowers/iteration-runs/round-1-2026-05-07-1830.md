## Aggregated suggested fixes (highest leverage first)

1. **Add `assumptions: list[str]` to ALL per-tool output schemas** + a hard requirement in the prompt: "Any behavioural claim you cannot verify from the supplied paths/intent MUST appear in `assumptions`, not in the rationale." (Addresses all 11 scenarios on `facts_vs_assumptions`.)
2. **Add `top_risks: list[{risk, why_specific_to_this_change, evidence_path}]` to `qa_decide_approach` output schema.** (Addresses 8 scenarios on `risk_based_focus`.)
3. **Add `suggested_tests: list[{name, technique, covers_risk}]` to `qa_decide_approach` output schema.** (Addresses 7 scenarios on `smallest_useful_test_set`.)
4. **Force domain anchoring in `_build_*_sampling_prompt`:** "Name the actual domain implied by target_paths (e.g. variant filtering, inventory feeding, bundle validation). Generic 'the service / the system' is not acceptable. Cite at least one concrete file/class/path in every recommendation." (Addresses 8 scenarios on `domain_specificity`.)
5. **Stop hard-coding generic risks in `_testing_answer` and `_risks_from_*`.** Move them to `missing_information`. Force the LLM to produce specific risks. (Addresses 7 scenarios on `no_generic_advice`.)
6. **Add `security_change` classification to `change_rules.yaml`** with security-specific must_consider, test_design_techniques, quality_characteristics, specialty_needs (OWASP ZAP, jwt_tool, fuzzing). Auto-elevate when `risk_notes` mention auth/payment/encryption. (Addresses security-token-refresh + future critical-path scenarios.)
7. **Promote ISTQB principle citation to a per-tool hard requirement** in every prompt builder + the system prompt's standing rules. (Addresses 3 scenarios + cross-cutting.)
8. **Detect strategy-shaped questions in `qa_answer_testing_question`** and emit a routing hint to `qa_create_test_plan` / `strategy-orchestration` instead of answering as Q&A. (Addresses generic.how-to-test-this-service.)
9. **Surface specialty pairings in `qa_decide_approach` schema as objects** `{specialty, tool}` rather than free-text strings, and add spike-context examples (property-based testing, financial-precision). (Addresses specialty_awareness gaps.)
